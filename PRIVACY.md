# Privacy policy


Effective upon deployment of this version. Operator: u/realsmurph; support and
removal requests: https://www.reddit.com/message/compose/?to=realsmurph .
This private app processes public listing text from the configured hardware-sale communities (default: r/homelabsales and r/hardwareswap), IDs, timestamps,
author names and flair to identify relevant hardware and exclusions. Selected
listing titles/excerpts, prices, locations, links and timestamps are transmitted to
the owner's Discord channel. Discord and Reddit process data under their own
policies. Redis stores the operational metadata described above; Devvit stores
configuration and the encrypted webhook secret. Data is not sold, used for ads,
or sent to an AI provider. There are no analytics or external account-linking flows.

Known Discord copies are deleted when the app observes removal/deletion, when
filters exclude the listing, or after 24 hours plus cleanup delay. Technical outages
and ambiguous deliveries can require manual deletion by the operator. Source
content is not backed up. Contact the operator with a listing link for removal
or privacy concerns. Disabling the app retains cleanup access until messages are
removed; uninstall only after verifying cleanup. People with access to the Discord
channel can see alerts; the operator should keep that audience restricted.
